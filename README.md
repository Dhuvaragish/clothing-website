# VÉRÉ — Maison de Mode Paris
### *The Art of Timeless Dressing*

A luxury fashion brand identity and editorial digital experience inspired by European old-money fashion heritage, Parisian elegance, and quiet luxury.

---

## 🏛️ Brand Identity

* **Brand Name**: **VÉRÉ** (pronounced *veh-ray*)
* **Tagline**: *"The Art of Timeless Dressing"*
* **Brand Persona**: Understated luxury, European fashion heritage, architectural drape, minimal sophistication, noble raw fibers, and generational longevity over seasonal fast-fashion trends.
* **Palette Philosophy**: Warm Beige and Ivory backgrounds grounded by Deep Espresso Brown typography and Champagne Gold accents.

---

---

## 🖋️ Typography

* **Editorial Headings & Brand Wordmark**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700 / Italic)
* **Navigation, Functional UI & Body Text**: `Montserrat` (Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600)
* **Editorial Styling**: Oversized titles, generous letter tracking (`0.16em` – `0.35em`), and balanced line layouts.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

* **100% Pure HTML5 & CSS3**: Zero JavaScript dependencies for maximum performance, security, and cleanliness.
* **Pure-CSS Interactive Drawers**:
  * **Navigation Drawer**: Left slideout drawer with curated collection indices.
  * **Search Overlay**: Top modal with curated luxury inquiries.
  * **Mini-Bag Drawer**: Right slideout showing curated atelier selection and subtotal.
* **Hero Campaign Banner**: Editorial full-viewport hero featuring high-fashion photography in a European architectural setting with a custom light-to-dark scrim.
* **Infinite Pure-CSS Marquee Ticker**: Continuous horizontal ethos ticker with soft vignette edge masks (`mask-image`).
* **Symmetric Brand Pillars**: Centered cards highlighting Noble Raw Fibers, Architectural Poise, Atelier Heritage, and Generational Longevity.
* **Curated Product Catalog**: Editorial cards with fabric provenance, Euro (€) pricing, and hover-activated quick add triggers.
* **Lookbook & Atelier Manifesto**: Magazine-style editorial spread featuring Creative Director commentary.
* **Private Client Concierge**: Accessible consultation appointment form with full semantic markup.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Clone or download this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Dhuvaragish/clothing-website.git
   ```
2. Open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
   *No build steps, node_modules, or server setup required.*
